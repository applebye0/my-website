I’m a senior studying Mechanical Engineering, with a minor in Economics, at the University of Colorado Boulder. I study human-robot interaction, especially how robots can behave in socially intelligent ways when they share everyday spaces with people. I’ve done research in the Interaction Research Lab at Cornell Tech and the HIRO Group at CU Boulder.

Research

  • Interaction Research Lab, Cornell Tech · since May 2026
    • Field studies of a trash-barrel robot that combines Wizard-of-Oz control with vision-language models for social intelligence. I ran its campus café deployments and analyzed how people responded to its social behaviors and errors.
  • Human Interaction and Robotics (HIRO) Group, University of Colorado Boulder · since September 2026
    • Developing expressive motion for a robot arm so it can convey emotions, focusing on hesitancy. The goal is for a robot's movements to communicate its uncertainty clearly to the people working alongside it.
